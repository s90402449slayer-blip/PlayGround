HOW TO PLAY

1. BuildTap any glowing pad on the island, then pick a unit. Or press 1–5 and tap a pad.
2. DefendCamera, Speaker, TV, and Titan units automatically attack toilets marching along the road.
3. UpgradeTap a deployed unit to upgrade it up to Level 3 or sell it for 70% back.
4. SurviveStop every toilet before it reaches the Camera HQ. Every leak damages your base.
TIP: Boss toilets appear every 5 waves. Save coins for Titan units and upgrades.
